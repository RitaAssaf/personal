
  How to do a square
  _______________________________________________________________________________________________________________

  Wrong:

  graph {

        1 [label="1"];
        2 [label="2"];
        3 [label="3"];
        4 [label="4"];


        1 -- 2;
        2 -- 3;
        3 -- 4;
        4 -- 1;

   }




Correct:

   strict graph
{
	1 [label=1]
	2 [label=2]
	3 [label=3]
	4 [label=4]

	1 -- 4 [angle=90]
	2 -- 3 [angle=90]

	rank=same {1 -- 2 [angle=0]}
	rank=same {4 -- 3 [angle=0]}
}
